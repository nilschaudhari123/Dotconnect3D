# Privacy policy placeholder

Color Path 3D stores puzzle progress, settings, coin balance, and purchase entitlements on the device. Progress records are signed so casual edits are rejected.

The game can send gameplay analytics events (level started, level completed, hint used, rewarded ad watched, purchase completed, daily challenge, achievement unlocked). Those events do not include your name, contacts, photos, or precise location.

Advertisements, Play Billing, Remote Config, Crashlytics, and optional Play Games leaderboards use Google services when a network is available. Previously downloaded puzzles, scores, settings, and earned hints work offline.
